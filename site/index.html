What the Busters do

One bot. One sacred duty: making sure nobody in your group skips 4pm chai ever again.

📢

Daily 4pm ping

Every day at 4:00 PM IST sharp, the bot screams CHAI TIME into the group. No snooze button. No excuses.

➕

+chai logging

Brewed your chai? Reply +chai. One log per person per day — the bot counts, and the bot remembers.

🏆

Monthly leaderboard

/leaderboard shows this month's standings with medals. Glory is quantified in chais.

🔥

Sunday roast

Every Sunday at 9pm IST, the bot publishes a brutally honest report on your group's brewing habits.

👑

Champion crowning

At 11:55pm on the last day of the month, the top brewer is crowned Chai Champion. Immortality, sort of.

🗄️

Zero-config storage

Plain SQLite. No database server, no setup, no drama. Your chai history lives in one little file.
