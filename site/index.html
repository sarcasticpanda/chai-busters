Three steps, no spreadsheet

From group chat to chai club.

Invite the bot once. It remembers each group's count separately and does the daily reminding for you.

01

Bring the bot in

Open @Kadak_chai_ahhh_bot, add it to a Telegram group, and send /start.

02

Log the cups

After brewing, send +chai for one cup or +chai 4 for four. One daily entry per person; send a number again to update that day's total.

03

Check the hisaab

Use /today, /groupstats, and /leaderboard. The bot posts the daily nudge, weekly report, and month-end crown automatically.

Keep it in the chat

A few words do the work.

The bot responds inside the group. Add it to another group and that chat gets a separate ledger.

/startSet up this group and show the quick guide.
+chai 4Log today's four cups and one brew round.
/todaySee who has brewed today.
/groupstatsSee members, brewers, rounds, and total cups.
/leaderboardSee this month's group rankings.
/championFind the most recent monthly champion.
